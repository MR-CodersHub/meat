FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pause or skip a delivery?
Yes! You can skip or pause your subscription at any time from your Subscriber Dashboard. We just need 48 hours notice before your next scheduled delivery date.
How is the meat kept fresh during delivery?
Every order is packed in our eco-friendly wool-lined insulated boxes with dry ice pellets. This keeps your meat safely below 40°F for up to 24 hours after delivery — even in summer heat.
What if I'm not satisfied with my order?
We stand behind every box 100%. If you're not satisfied for any reason, contact us within 48 hours and we'll either replace the item or issue a full credit to your account — no questions asked.
Do you deliver to my area?
We currently deliver to 8 states in the Pacific Northwest and West Coast. Enter your zip code during checkout to confirm availability. We're expanding to new regions every quarter.
Can I change my subscription plan?
Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade or completely change your subscription plan at any time from your dashboard. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.
