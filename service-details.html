The FarmFresh Signature Meat Box

Our Meat Box Subscription is the easiest way to fill your freezer with honest, premium-quality cuts from local farms you can trust. Every box is curated by our head butcher Marcus Webb, a 20-year veteran of artisan butchery with deep relationships across our farm network.

Each delivery arrives in our eco-friendly insulated packaging, vacuum-sealed to lock in peak freshness, with handwritten butcher notes and recipe cards for every cut included.

Why Grass-Fed?

Grass-fed beef contains up to 5× more omega-3 fatty acids, 2× more conjugated linoleic acid (CLA), and significantly higher levels of Vitamin E and beta-carotene compared to grain-fed beef. It's not just better for you — it's better for the environment.
