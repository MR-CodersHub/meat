Quick Answers

Common Questions Before You Contact

How do I pause or cancel my subscription?
Log into your Subscriber Dashboard and navigate to "Subscription Settings." You can pause, skip, or cancel with immediate effect — no fees, no phone call required. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.
My order didn't arrive. What should I do?
First, check your tracking link in the confirmation email. If the delivery shows as delivered but you can't find it, contact us immediately via phone or live chat. We'll investigate and either reship or issue a full refund within 24 hours.
I'm interested in wholesale pricing for my restaurant. Who do I contact?
Email our wholesale team directly at wholesale@farmfreshmeats.com or use the contact form above selecting "Bulk / Wholesale." A dedicated account manager will respond within 4 business hours with pricing and terms.
How do I change the cuts in my subscription box?
Log in to your dashboard and visit "Customize My Box." You can change your cuts, quantities, and protein types any time before your weekly cutoff (Wednesday 11:59 PM PT for Friday deliveries).
