Who We Are

Built on a Simple Promise:
Better Meat, Honestly Raised

FarmFresh Meats was founded by Tom and Lisa Hansen after they struggled to find quality, honest meat for their growing family. Frustrated by supermarket labels and factory farming, they drove to a local farm — and what they found changed everything.

They partnered with 12 local farms to create a direct-to-consumer subscription. Today, we work with 500+ farms across the Pacific Northwest, delivering to 12,000+ families every week. Our mission hasn't changed: give every family access to honest, transparently sourced meat from farmers they can trust.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

These aren't marketing slogans — they're the standards we hold ourselves to every single day.

Radical Transparency

Every package includes the farm name, animal ID and harvest date. We believe you deserve to know exactly where your food comes from.

Sustainable Agriculture

We only partner with farms that use regenerative practices — improving soil health, sequestering carbon and protecting watersheds.

Humane Animal Welfare

All animals are raised on open pasture with full freedom of movement. We do not work with farms that use confinement or battery cage systems.

Community First

We pay farmers 30% more than commodity markets. Thriving local farms mean stronger local communities and a more resilient food system.

Uncompromising Quality

Every batch is taste-tested by our team. We'd rather deliver less than deliver something that doesn't meet our standards.

Zero Waste Commitment

Our packaging is 100% recyclable. We're working toward carbon-neutral delivery by 2027 through EV fleet and carbon offset programs.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

2016

FarmFresh Founded

Tom and Lisa Hansen launch FarmFresh Meats from their garage, partnering with 12 local farms and serving 80 founding subscribers in Portland.

2018

1,000 Subscribers Milestone

After viral word-of-mouth and a feature in Bon Appétit magazine, we hit 1,000 subscribers and opened our first dedicated processing facility.

2020

Certified Humane Partnership

We became the first meat subscription service in Oregon to achieve full Certified Humane® accreditation across our entire farm network.

2022

Pacific Northwest Expansion

Expanded to 8 states, onboarded 300+ new farm partners, and launched the Athlete Box and Bulk programs to serve new audiences.

2026

12,000 Families & Growing

Today FarmFresh serves over 12,000 families weekly. We're working toward carbon-neutral delivery and partnering with 500+ farms.
